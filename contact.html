FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (1-2 days) is available at checkout for an additional fee. Free shipping on orders over $100.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. Items must be unworn and in original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order by logging into your account.

Are all products authentic?

Yes! Every product is 100% authentic, sourced directly from authorized manufacturers and distributors. We guarantee authenticity or full refund.
