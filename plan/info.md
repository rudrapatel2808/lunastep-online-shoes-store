# Online Shoes Selling System

## Project Overview
The **Online Shoes Selling System** is a modern, feature-rich web application designed to facilitate the online retail of footwear. It provides a seamless shopping experience for customers while offering robust management tools for administrators and inventory staff.

## Core Objectives
1. **Enhanced Shopping Experience:** Provide a highly responsive, aesthetically pleasing interface (using glassmorphism) for browsing, searching, and purchasing shoes.
2. **Intelligent Recommendations:** Utilize AI-driven insights to recommend products based on user behavior and preferences.
3. **Efficient Inventory Management:** Track stock levels in real-time, generate low-stock alerts, and maintain a history of inventory movements.
4. **Automated Workflows:** Leverage n8n for automating routine tasks such as email notifications (order confirmation, abandoned carts) and report generation.

## Key Modules
1. **Customer Module:** Registration, login, product catalog, search/filter, cart, checkout, order tracking, wishlist, and reviews.
2. **Administrator Module:** Dashboard, user management, product/category management, order processing, returns, coupons, and analytics.
3. **Inventory Module:** Stock updates, low-stock monitoring, barcode scanning integration, and stock history.

## Target Audience
- **Customers:** Individuals looking to purchase high-quality footwear online.
- **Store Owners & Managers:** Administrators who need to oversee sales, inventory, and customer relations.
- **Inventory Staff:** Personnel responsible for maintaining stock accuracy.
